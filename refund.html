REFUND POLICY (Kahaniyon)

Aakhri badlav: November 2026

Read in English

Ye policy coins ki kharid ke baare mein hai.

1. MOOL NIYAM

Coins digital (virtual) saman hain. Coins ki payment hone aur coins aapke account mein judne ke baad aam taur par refund nahi hota. Kharch ho chuke coins wapas nahi hote.

2. JAB REFUND YA COINS MILENGE

Ye haalat mein hum jaanch ke baad ya to sahi coins jod denge ya paise wapas karenge:

  • Aapke paise kat gaye par coins nahi mile.
  • Ek hi payment do baar kat gayi.
  • Payment ki rakam ke hisaab se coins kam mile.
  • App ki tech galti se coins galat judne ya ghatne.

3. AI KA JAWAB NA AANE PAR

Message bhejne par coins nahi kat'te. Coins sirf tab kat'te hain jab AI ka jawab sach mein aa jaye, aur har jawab ke 10 coins lagte hain (aapka message aur AI ka jawab milkar ek message). Agar jawab nahi aata, to us jawab ke koi coins nahi kat'te, isliye refund ki zaroorat nahi padti.

4. JIN CHEEZON PAR REFUND NAHI

  • Kharch ho chuke coins.
  • Free coins ya muft mile coins (inka koi paisa nahi hota).
  • Galat ya nakli UTR bhejna, ya UTR jo pehle istemal ho chuka ho.
  • Sirf man badalne ya "ab nahi chahiye" ki wajah se.
  • Aapke account ko sharton ke ulanghan par band karna.
  • Aapke dwara galti se kisi aur UPI ID ya account mein payment karna.

5. REFUND KAISE MAANGEIN

Payment ke 7 din ke andar Profile mein "Support" kholkar diye gaye email ya WhatsApp link par ye jaankari bhejo:

  • UTR (transaction ID)
  • Payment ka screenshot
  • Aapka login email ya phone number
  • Kaunsa coin pack kharida tha aur kya dikkat hai

6. KITNA SAMAY LAGEGA

Hum aapka request 7 din ke andar dekhenge. Agar refund manzoor hota hai, to paise usi tareeke (UPI) se wapas aate hain jisse payment hui thi. Bank ke hisaab se 5 se 10 kaam ke din lag sakte hain.

7. GALAT DAAVE

Jhoote ya dhokhe wale refund request par hum account band kar sakte hain.

8. AAPKE ADHIKAR

Is policy se Bharat ke Consumer Protection kanoon ke tahat aapke adhikar prabhavit nahi hote. 7 din ki samay seema sirf hamari is policy ke tahat refund request bhejne ke liye hai. Ye samay seema khatam hone par bhi kanoon ke tahat aapke adhikar barkarar rehte hain.

9. SAMPARK

Koi sawaal ho to app ke Profile mein "Support" mein diye gaye email ya WhatsApp link par hume likho.
