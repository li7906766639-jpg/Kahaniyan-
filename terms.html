TERMS OF SERVICE (Kahaniyon)

Aakhri badlav: November 2026

Read in English

Kahaniyon app istemal karne se pehle ye sharten dhyan se padho. App istemal karne ka matlab hai ki aap inhe maante ho.

1. UMAR

Ye app sirf 18 saal ya usse zyada umar ke logon ke liye hai.

2. AAPKA ACCOUNT

  • Aap Google ya Email-Password se login karte ho. Sahi jaankari do aur apna login surakshit rakho.
  • Aapke account se jo bhi hota hai, uski zimmedari aapki hai.
  • Ek insaan ek hi account banaye. Free coins lene ke liye nakli ya kai account banana mana hai.

3. SEWA KYA HAI

Kahaniyon AI se chalne wali kaalpanik kahaniyon wali app hai. Characters asli insaan nahi hain aur kahaniyan asli logon ya ghatnaon se nahi judi hain. AI galti kar sakta hai ya ajeeb jawab de sakta hai. Ye koi daktari, kanooni ya paise ki salah nahi hai. Kisi sankat mein asli logon ya sahi sansthaon se madad lo.

4. COINS

  • Coins app ke andar ka virtual saman hain. Inki koi naqad keemat nahi hai, inhe kisi ko diya ya becha nahi ja sakta.
  • Message bhejne par coins nahi kat'te. Coins sirf tab kat'te hain jab AI ka jawab aa jaye, aur har aise jawab ke 10 coins lagte hain. Aapka message aur AI ka jawab milkar ek message ginta hai, yani ek message ke kul 10 coins.
  • Dobara banao aur aage badhao par bhi AI ka naya jawab aane par 10 coins lagte hain.
  • Agar AI ka jawab nahi aata, to us jawab ke koi coins nahi kat'te.
  • Naye users ko kuch free coins mil sakte hain. Free coins ki ginti hum badal sakte hain.
  • Account band hone par bache hue coins khatam ho sakte hain (jaisa Refund Policy mein likha hai, uske alawa).

5. PAYMENT

Coins UPI se kharide jaate hain. Payment ke baad UTR (transaction ID) bhejna hota hai. Jaanch ke baad coins judte hain. Refund ke liye Refund Policy dekho.

6. KYA MANA HAI

  • Kanoon ke khilaaf koi bhi kaam.
  • Nafrat, gaali-galoch, dhamki, pareshan karna ya kisi ki pehchaan chura kar dhokha dena.
  • Bachchon se jude koi bhi yaun ya nazuk content.
  • Doosron ki nijee jaankari bina ijazat daalna.
  • App ko hack karna, tod-phod karna, data copy karna ya reverse engineering.
  • Dhokhe se coins lena, jaise nakli accounts, nakli UTR ya payment mein gadbad.
  • AI ko galat content likhne ke liye majboor karne ki koshish, ya spam.

7. AAPKA CONTENT AUR CHAT KA ISTEMAL

Aap jo messages likhte ho wo aapke hain. Aap hume unhe sirf in kaamon ke liye istemal karne ki ijazat dete ho:

  • App chalane ke liye: AI ka jawab banane ke liye aapke messages AI service ko bheje jaate hain.
  • Backup ke liye: aapki chats aur Story Memory cloud mein save karna, taaki naye phone par wapas mil sake.
  • Suraksha ke liye: galat istemal, dhokha ya sharton ke ulanghan ki jaanch aur support dene ke liye, jinke liye hum aapki chats dekh sakte hain.
  • App ko behtar banane ke liye: app ki galtiyan dhundna aur unhe theek karna.

Hum aapki chats ko vigyapan ke liye istemal nahi karte aur kisi ko bechte nahi (Privacy Policy dekho). Chat mein apne password, bank ya Aadhaar jaisi nazuk jaankari mat likho.

8. AI KA CONTENT

AI ke jawab kaalpanik hote hain. Hum nahi kehte ki wo sahi, poore ya aapke kaam ke layak hain.

9. HAMARI MILKIYAT

App, uska design, kahaniyan aur characters hamare ya hamare licensor ke hain. Aap unhe bina likhit ijazat ke copy ya dobara istemal nahi kar sakte.

10. SEWA MEIN BADLAV

Hum app mein feature badal, rok ya band kar sakte hain, maintenance ya kisi tech wajah se. Hum har samay bina ruke chalne ka vaada nahi karte.

11. ACCOUNT BAND KARNA

Agar aap in sharton ko todte ho, to hum aapka account rok ya band kar sakte hain. Aap bhi kabhi bhi app istemal karna band kar sakte ho aur apna data mitane ki maang Support se kar sakte ho.

12. ZIMMEDARI KI SEEMA

App "jaisi hai waisi" di jaati hai. Kanoon ki seema mein, hum kisi bhi paroksh nuksaan ya data kho jaane ke liye zimmedar nahi hain. Kisi bhi haalat mein hamari adhiktam zimmedari us rakam tak hogi jo aapne pichhle 3 mahine mein app ko di ho. Is se aapke Consumer Protection kanoon ke adhikar prabhavit nahi hote.

13. KANOON

In sharton par Bharat ke kanoon lagu honge. Koi vivad ho to pehle Support se baat karo. Na suljhe to Bharat ki adalat uska nirnay karengi.

14. SHARTON MEIN BADLAV

Hum ye sharten badal sakte hain. Naya version isi jagah dikhega. Badlav ke baad app istemal karte rehna naye version ko maanna hai.

15. SAMPARK

Koi sawaal ho to app ke Profile mein "Support" mein diye gaye email ya WhatsApp link par hume likho.
