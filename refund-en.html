REFUND POLICY (Kahaniyon)

Last updated: November 2026

Roman Hindi version

This policy is about buying coins.

1. BASIC RULE

Coins are digital (virtual) items. Once the payment is made and the coins are added to your account, they are generally not refundable. Coins that have been used cannot be returned.

2. WHEN YOU WILL GET A REFUND OR COINS

In these situations, after checking, we will either add the correct coins or return your money:

  • Your money was deducted but you did not receive the coins.
  • The same payment was deducted twice.
  • You received fewer coins than the payment amount should give.
  • Coins were wrongly added or deducted because of a technical error in the app.

3. WHEN THE AI'S REPLY DOES NOT ARRIVE

No coins are deducted when you send a message. Coins are deducted only when the AI's reply actually arrives, and each reply costs 10 coins (your message and the AI's reply together count as one message). If the reply does not arrive, no coins are deducted for it, so no refund is needed.

4. WHAT IS NOT REFUNDED

  • Coins that have been used.
  • Free coins (they have no money value).
  • Sending a wrong or fake UTR, or a UTR that has already been used.
  • Changing your mind, or "I don't want it anymore".
  • Closing your account for breaking the terms.
  • Paying by mistake to some other UPI ID or account.

5. HOW TO ASK FOR A REFUND

Within 7 days of the payment, open "Support" in your Profile and send the following to the email or WhatsApp link given there:

  • UTR (transaction ID)
  • A screenshot of the payment
  • Your login email or phone number
  • Which coin pack you bought and what the problem is

6. HOW LONG IT TAKES

We will look at your request within 7 days. If a refund is approved, the money is returned the same way (UPI) as the payment. Depending on your bank, it may take 5 to 10 working days.

7. FALSE CLAIMS

We may close accounts for false or fraudulent refund requests.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

This policy does not affect your rights under India's consumer protection laws. The 7-day period applies only to sending a refund request under this policy. Your rights under the law remain even after this period ends.

9. CONTACT

If you have any questions, write to us using the email or WhatsApp link given under "Support" in the app's Profile.
