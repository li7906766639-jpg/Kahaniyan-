PRIVACY POLICY (Kahaniyon)

Aakhri badlav: November 2026

Read in English

Hum aapki privacy ka dhyan rakhte hain. Yahan saaf likha hai ki hum kaun si jaankari lete hain, kyun lete hain, kahan rakhte hain, aur aap use kaise control kar sakte ho.

1. HUM KAUN SI JAANKARI LETE HAIN

  • Login ki jaankari: Google ya Email-Password se login karne par aapka email, naam aur ek khaas user ID (Firebase Authentication ke zariye).
  • Jo aap khud daalte ho: naam, phone number (ye verify nahi hota) aur gender (optional).
  • Chat: aapke bheje messages, AI ke jawab aur "Story Memory" (purani kahani ka chhota saar jo AI ko yaad rakhne mein madad karta hai).
  • Coins aur payment: coins ki ginti, aur coins kharidne par payment ka record (naam, email, phone, pack, rakam aur UPI transaction ID/UTR). Agar aap payment ya refund ke liye Support ko payment ka screenshot bhejte ho (email ya WhatsApp se), to wo screenshot bhi hamare paas save hota hai.
  • Report: agar aap koi report bhejte ho to wo.
  • Ginti ke aankde: kahaniyon ki lokpriyata ginne ke liye chats ki ginti (isse aapki pehchaan nahi judi hoti).

2. HUM ISE KYUN ISTEMAL KARTE HAIN

  • App chalane, login aur coins sambhalne ke liye.
  • Aapki chat aur Story Memory ko surakshit rakhne aur naye phone par wapas dene ke liye.
  • Payment aur refund ki jaanch aur support dene ke liye.
  • App ko surakshit rakhne aur galat istemal rokne ke liye.

3. DATA KAHAN RAKHA JAATA HAI

  • Aapke phone mein: chats, settings aur theme.
  • Cloud mein (Google Firebase): login, coins, payments, reports aur chat ka backup (messages aur Story Memory).
  • Chat ka backup app ke malik ko support, suraksha aur galat istemal ki jaanch ke liye dikh sakta hai.
  • Payment ka screenshot (agar aap Support ko bhejte ho): hamare email ya WhatsApp mein, jahan aapne bheja.

4. DUSRI SERVICES JO ISTEMAL HOTI HAIN

  • AI jawab banane ke liye aapke messages AI service ko bheje jaate hain (jaise Google Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek ya OpenRouter, jo bhi app use kare). Un par unki apni privacy policy lagti hai.
  • Awaaz se bolna ya sunna istemal karo to aapki awaaz ya text phone ki speech service ya awaaz banane wali service (jaise ElevenLabs) tak ja sakta hai.
  • Login aur database ke liye Google Firebase.

5. PAYMENT

Coins ki payment UPI se app ke bahar hoti hai. Hum aapka UPI PIN, bank ya card ki jaankari kabhi nahi lete. Hum sirf transaction ID, naam, email, phone, pack ka record aur (agar aap bhejo to) payment ka screenshot rakhte hain taaki payment ki jaanch aur refund ho sake.

6. HUM DATA BECHTE NAHI

Hum aapki jaankari kisi ko bechte nahi aur vigyapan ke liye share nahi karte. Sirf upar likhi zaroori services (jaise AI aur Firebase) ko utna hi jaata hai jitna app chalane ke liye chahiye. Kanoon ya adalat ke kehne par jaankari dena pad sakta hai.

7. DATA KITNE DIN RAKHA JAATA HAI AUR ACCOUNT DELETE HONE PAR KYA HOTA HAI

Jab tak aapka account aur app istemal mein hai, tab tak aapka data rakha jaata hai.

  • Aap "Account Settings" mein "Erase All Data" se apne phone ki chats mita sakte ho aur logout ho jaate ho.
  • Account delete karne ki maang Support mein karo. Account delete hone par hum ye mita denge: aapka account aur login ki jaankari, aapka naam, phone number aur gender, aapki chats aur Story Memory (cloud backup), aur aapke coins ka record.
  • Ye record hamare paas rahenge: payment ke record (naam, email, phone, pack, rakam, UTR aur payment ka screenshot) aur doosre zaroori record jinhe kanoon, hisaab-kitaab (accounting) aur payment ki jaanch ke liye rakhna zaroori hai. Inhe sirf in kaamon ke liye rakha jaata hai.
  • Ye record kitne samay tak rakhe jaate hain, ye kanoon aur zaroorat ke hisaab se tay hota hai.

8. SURAKSHA

Hum Firebase ke suraksha niyam aur surakshit connection (HTTPS) istemal karte hain. Par koi bhi system 100% surakshit nahi hota. Apna login surakshit rakho aur chat mein apne password, bank ya Aadhaar jaisi nazuk jaankari mat likho.

9. BACHCHE

Ye app 18 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ke liye nahi hai.

10. AAPKE ADHIKAR

Aap apni jaankari dekhne, sudharne ya mitane ki maang kar sakte ho. Iske liye app ke Profile mein "Support" kholkar diye gaye email ya WhatsApp link par hume likho. Jo record kanoon, accounting ya payment ki jaanch ke liye rakhna zaroori hai (hissa 7 dekho), unhe rakhna padega, par baaki jaankari mita di jaayegi.

11. KAHANIYAN KAALPANIK HAIN

App ki kahaniyan aur AI ke jawab kaalpanik hain aur asli logon ya ghatnaon se nahi judi hain.

12. POLICY MEIN BADLAV

Hum ye policy badal sakte hain. Naya version isi jagah dikhega. App istemal karte rehna naye version ko maanna hai.

13. SAMPARK

Koi sawaal ho to app ke Profile mein "Support" mein diye gaye email ya WhatsApp link par hume likho.
