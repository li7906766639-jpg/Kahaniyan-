PRIVACY POLICY (Kahaniyon)

Last updated: November 2026

Roman Hindi version

We care about your privacy. This explains clearly what information we collect, why we collect it, where we keep it, and how you can control it.

1. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Login information: When you log in with Google or Email-Password, your email, name and a unique user ID (through Firebase Authentication).
  • What you enter yourself: your name, phone number (this is not verified) and gender (optional).
  • Chats: the messages you send, the AI's replies and the "Story Memory" (a short summary of the earlier story that helps the AI remember).
  • Coins and payments: your coin balance and, when you buy coins, a payment record (name, email, phone, pack, amount and UPI transaction ID/UTR). If you send a screenshot of your payment to Support (by email or WhatsApp) for payment checking or a refund, that screenshot is also saved with us.
  • Reports: any report you send.
  • Counts: counts of chats, used to see which stories are popular (these are not linked to your identity).

2. WHY WE USE IT

  • To run the app, handle login and manage coins.
  • To keep your chats and Story Memory safe and give them back on a new phone.
  • To check payments and refunds and give support.
  • To keep the app safe and prevent misuse.

3. WHERE DATA IS STORED

  • On your phone: chats, settings and theme.
  • In the cloud (Google Firebase): login, coins, payments, reports and the chat backup (messages and Story Memory).
  • The chat backup may be visible to the app owner for support, safety and checking misuse.
  • A payment screenshot (if you send one to Support): in our email or WhatsApp, where you sent it.

4. OTHER SERVICES WE USE

  • To create AI replies, your messages are sent to an AI service (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek or OpenRouter, whichever the app uses). Their own privacy policies apply.
  • If you use voice input or voice playback, your voice or text may go to your phone's speech service or a voice service (such as ElevenLabs).
  • Google Firebase is used for login and the database.

5. PAYMENTS

Coin payments are made through UPI outside the app. We never collect your UPI PIN, bank or card details. We only keep the transaction ID, name, email, phone, the pack record and (if you send it) a screenshot of the payment, so that the payment can be checked and refunds can be handled.

6. WE DO NOT SELL DATA

We do not sell your information to anyone and do not share it for advertising. It goes only to the necessary services listed above (such as AI and Firebase), and only as much as is needed to run the app. We may have to share information if required by law or by a court.

7. HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT AND WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AN ACCOUNT IS DELETED

Your data is kept as long as your account and the app are in use.

  • From the chat menu you can use "Delete Chat" or "Replay" (restart the story) to remove a chat from both your phone and the cloud backup. If you delete a single message, it is removed from your phone and, when you are online, from the cloud backup too.
  • In "Account Settings", "Erase All Data" deletes all your chats and Story Memory from both your phone and the cloud backup, and logs you out. It does not delete your account, coins or payment records.
  • For "Delete Chat" or "Replay", if you are offline the cloud deletion is completed later when you are online. "Erase All Data" needs internet.
  • After deletion, an empty marker (the story name and the deletion time) may remain in the backup, not your conversation.
  • Ask Support to delete your account. When your account is deleted, we will delete: your account and login information, your name, phone number and gender, your chats and Story Memory (cloud backup), and your coin record.
  • These records will stay with us: payment records (name, email, phone, pack, amount, UTR and payment screenshot) and other necessary records that we must keep for legal, accounting and payment verification purposes. They are kept only for these purposes.
  • How long these records are kept is decided according to the law and need.

8. SECURITY

We use Firebase security rules and secure connections (HTTPS). But no system is 100% secure. Keep your login safe and do not write sensitive information such as passwords, bank details or Aadhaar numbers in chats.

9. CHILDREN

This app is not for children under 18.

10. YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. To do this, open "Support" in the app's Profile and write to us at the email or WhatsApp link given there. Records that must be kept for legal, accounting or payment verification purposes (see section 7) must stay with us, but the rest of your information will be deleted. You can also delete your chats yourself from the app (see section 7).

11. THE STORIES ARE FICTIONAL

The app's stories and AI replies are fictional and are not connected to real people or events.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may change this policy. The new version will appear here. Continuing to use the app means you accept the new version.

13. CONTACT

If you have any questions, write to us using the email or WhatsApp link given under "Support" in the app's Profile.
